Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

At Up4Growth.CH, operated by SixthPin GmbH, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under Swiss data protection law (nDSG) and GDPR where applicable.

1. Data Controller

SixthPin GmbH
Basel, Switzerland
Email: contact@up4growth.ch

2. What Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Data you provide directly:

  • Name, email, phone number
  • Billing information
  • Booking details
  • Messages sent through forms or email

2.2 Automatically collected data:

  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Browsing behavior (pages visited, time spent)
  • Cookies (see section 7)

2.3 During coaching/training:

  • Notes or content shared voluntarily during coaching sessions.

3. Purpose of Processing

  • Managing bookings and payments
  • Delivering coaching and training services
  • Communication related to our services
  • Improving our website and offerings
  • Legal compliance and invoicing
  • Marketing communication (only with consent)

4. Legal Basis

  • Consent
  • Performance of a contract
  • Legitimate interests (service improvement, analytics)
  • Legal obligations (accounting and tax)

5. Data Sharing

  • Payment service providers
  • Invoicing and accounting tools
  • IT hosting partners
  • Email and communication platforms

We do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising.

Data is stored in Switzerland or the EU whenever possible.

6. Data Retention

  • As long as required for service delivery
  • According to business needs
  • As required by Swiss law (10 years for invoices)

7. Cookies and Tracking

Up4Growth.CH may use cookies for:

  • Website functionality
  • Analytics tools

You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Our analytics tools anonymize IP addresses.

8. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Request correction
  • Request deletion
  • Withdraw consent
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability (where applicable)

Requests can be sent to contact@up4growth.ch.

9. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

10. Third-Country Transfers

If we use tools that transfer data outside Switzerland/EU, this is done with:

  • Adequacy decisions
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Equivalent protections under GDPR/nDSG

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: contact@up4growth.ch
